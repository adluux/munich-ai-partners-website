AI vision & fundraising

Client: FastReview

€1M+ raised on the back of a sharper AI-first product vision

Delivered in 10 weeks

The problem

FastReview had strong traction and credible customers, but no AI-first product vision, and a pitch deck too generic to show investors how strong the business actually was.

What we did

We clarified FastReview's core value proposition, then redefined the product vision for an AI-first market — stronger automation, better insights, a more scalable operating model — and restructured the pitch deck to lead with traction, customer logos, market relevance, and that AI-first growth path.

The result

FastReview raised over €1M in new funding, and came out of the process with a clearer product vision and a pitch deck that actually reflected its real traction and ambition, not a better-designed version of the same generic story.

Why it matters

A weak investor story doesn't just cost a company a round, it costs credibility with investors who've already seen the generic version of this pitch a dozen times. Fixing the vision before the deck is what made the new story credible, not just better produced.
