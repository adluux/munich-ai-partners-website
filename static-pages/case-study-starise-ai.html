Outreach capacity

Client: Starise AI

100x increase in outreach capacity, same team, same cost

Delivered in 2 weeks

The problem

Starise had a market-ready product but no scalable way to find the right customers, short of burning cash on lead lists or searching for businesses manually, one city at a time.

What we did

We built an internal tool that turns Google Maps into a targeted prospecting engine, letting Starise choose any city in the world, define the target segment, and filter by category, rating, and review volume to produce a usable lead list with contact details.

The result

Starise increased cold outreach capacity by roughly 100x with the same team and cost base, replacing a slow, manual, one-city-at-a-time search with a repeatable process the team can now run for any city or segment in minutes, and use to test which segments and geographies convert best.

Why it matters

For an early-stage company, the real constraint usually isn't whether the product works, it's whether you can find customers fast enough to prove that it does before the money runs out. This turned that race from a manual bottleneck into a system Starise controls.
