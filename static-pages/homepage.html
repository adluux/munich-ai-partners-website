How we work

We start with your problem, and we work until it is resolved.

The question is never "where can we apply AI." It is "what is holding this business back, and what is the most effective way to fix it."

01

Diagnose

We identify the specific problems worth solving, the ones costing the most, blocking your business from performing at its best, and creating the highest return when fixed. We go deep on every one. If none of them justify the engagement, we will tell you before any work begins.

02

Design the solution

The solution is built around the specific problem identified in the diagnosis, with AI applied where it genuinely adds value. Every proposal comes with concrete, measurable success metrics. This phase does not close until you have signed off on both the design and the metrics.

03

Execute

Hands-on delivery by senior people, from start to finish. No handoffs. We work in weekly iterations so you can see progress and give feedback throughout. We use AI actively in execution to deliver the highest quality in the most efficient way.

04

Measure

We measure success against the agreed metrics, not against a scope document or a timeline. The engagement is completed when the outcome is delivered.

This means we will tell you at the outset if we don't think the business case is strong enough to justify the engagement.
